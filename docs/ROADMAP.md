# What's next

## v1: the core desktop experience

- Redesigned player, dark/light themes and accents.
- Queue, bookmarks, chapters and quick adjustments.
- Home, video/music libraries, search, network and playlists.
- Compact browsing and a docked mini-player.
- Five settings pages with 101 controls.
- Shared Qt/QML source for macOS, Linux and Windows.

## v1 follow-ups

- Resolve Windows fresh-start delay and intermittent quit hang.
- Complete platform interaction testing and matched installer builds.
- Finish download packaging, source bundles and asset notices.

## v1.1: more control

- Equalizer, compressor and deeper audio/video panels.
- Redesigned Privacy, Controls, Video, Library, Network, Performance, Recording/Casting/Streaming and Extensions settings.
- Searchable keyboard shortcuts and richer shortcut feedback.
- Full welcome, theme and shortcut tour.

## Beyond

- Drag-and-drop player customization.
- Movies, Shows, Music and Work profiles.
- Per-folder and per-show track/filter presets.
- Batch subtitle and external audio matching.
- Online subtitles and subtitle links.
- Thumbnail previews while seeking and audio waveforms.
- Subtitle timeline with drag-to-sync.
- Floating picture-in-picture.
- Sleep timer and redesigned statistics.

The original VLC dialogs stay accessible while redesigned replacements are built. Roadmap versions describe priorities, not delivery dates.
